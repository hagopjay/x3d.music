# How the harmony was made and checked

Grades: **C** a command shows it, **L** a judgement, **S** not measured.

## The Turca (`score/harmony.py`, `score/turca.py`)

1. The key per stretch is a **hand-checked table** (`KEYS` in `score/turca.py`): lower case is minor. It started from the file's own key signatures (`*k[]` for A, B, G, H; `*k[f#c#g#]` for C to F, I, J) and the section labels, was compared with a Krumhansl-Schmuckler estimate per section and per 8-quarter window (L), and was then read against the chord labels below. Result: B and H open in C major for 8 quarters before A minor; E is A major for 16 quarters, then F sharp minor. The estimator alone misleads on sections that end on the dominant (A, D, G read as E minor and C sharp minor), which is why the table is by hand.
2. **Chord labels** (L): per beat, the sounding pitch classes weighted by overlap, plus these adjustments, each a fraction of the beat's total weight: a bonus (0.3) for a chord whose root is the bass, where the bass is the lowest **left-hand** note sounding (the right hand is never the bass); a bonus (0.2) for chords made only of notes of the key (natural, harmonic and melodic minor together); a bonus (0.1) for major and minor chords on the tonic, subdominant and dominant, and for the dominant seventh; a bonus (**STAY** = 0.3) for staying on the chord before, so that an oom-pah bar (a bass note, then two dyads above it) is one chord; and a penalty (0.7 per unit of weight) for notes outside the chord. A beat in which the left hand is silent (an upbeat, a rest) is not a chord change: it keeps the chord named just before it in the same key stretch, and the first beat of a stretch takes the key's tonic triad. Templates: major, minor, diminished triads; dominant seventh; diminished seventh. Neighbours with the same root and quality are merged whatever the bass; the figure (`6`, `64`, `7`) is read from the bass where the chord begins. Grace notes are not counted.
3. **Roman numerals** are read from the chord root against the key of the stretch, with inversion figures from the bass.
4. **Tension** (C for the arithmetic, `score/harmony.py`; the weights are the spec's starting values, "tune by eye"): the distance on the spiral array between the chord centre and the key centre. Measured on the Turca card (195 segments, grouped by Roman numeral): tonic 0.31 to 0.36 (87 segments), dominant 0.68 to 0.94 (66), subdominant 0.99 to 1.03 (22), supertonic 1.12 to 1.33 (14), submediant 0.99 to 1.31 (6).

What a person should check by eye in the first lit still: the light colour at the chord changes in A (A minor for 9 quarters, then the dominant E for 7) and in J (the coda alternates tonic, subdominant and dominant).

**What changed while P1 was built, and why (C for the counts, both versions were built; L for the reading).** The first labeller took the lowest sounding note of either hand as the bass and a staying bonus of 0.15, and merged only chords with the same bass. It gave 329 segments and two faults. The first beat of most sections was a chord that is not the tonic, because the left hand rests there and the right hand's lowest note was taken for the bass (G sharp diminished in A and G, a B seventh over A in C and F, B minor in D). And the first two bars of A flickered between A minor and C major on every second beat, because the left hand plays A, then C and E twice, and the dyad's lowest note was taken for the bass. With the left-hand-only bass, the rest rule, STAY = 0.3 and merging by chord, the same cards have 195 segments, every section's first beat is its tonic, and A reads A minor, then E major from bar 5 to the repeat. One-beat chords still remain (9 in the first pass of the Turca, found as an X-Y-X pattern one beat long): they read as the cadence iv, i6, iv, i64, V, i in B and H (3 each) and as neighbour or passing chords in E (3); I found no noise among them (L).

## The Ode (`HARM` in `score/ode.py`)

D and A with G for colour, written bar by bar for the 16-bar theme (half-bar chords; bar 11 gets a G on its middle two beats). The intro is a D pedal; the coda quotes the last phrase's harmony, then closes on A (two bars) and D (two bars). Nothing in the arrangement may sit against the melody; P2 decorates these chords (added 9ths, sus4) and the Score witness keeps checking that the melody is untouched.
